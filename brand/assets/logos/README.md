The Market's approved marks. All lettering is outlined, so the SVGs need no fonts and look the same everywhere. The PNGs (transparent) are for Canva, Word and other tools that can't take SVG.

- `emblem` (the Bay Badge) is the primary mark: posters, the footer, merch and the info tent. Minimum 120px / 1.25 in.
- `emblem-no-ribbon` goes in the site header, on avatars and on stamps. Minimum 72px.
- `icon` is the favicon, app icon and small watermark. Minimum 24px.
- `lockup` goes on light grounds and `lockup-reverse` on `deep` or `bay`. Use them in wide spaces and for co-branding. Minimum 200px wide.
- `night-market`, `winter-market` and `pop-up-market` are the sub-brand tickets. Always show the Bay Badge or the lockup somewhere on the same piece.

Clear space is about 12% of the badge's diameter on every side. Never recolour, stretch, crop or redraw a mark.
