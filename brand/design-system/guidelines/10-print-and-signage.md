# Print & signage

Signs are read from 3–10 metres away by people walking with their hands full. Give each piece one job, and build it like a screen print: flat colour, ink outlines, the awning or the wave.

## Formats

| Piece | Size | Anatomy |
|---|---|---|
| Season poster | 11 × 17 in | Awning edge across the top · Bay Badge centred, 45% of the width · "SATURDAYS BY THE LAKE" in League Gothic · every season date in a ticket strip · a QR code bottom right with the URL written under it · sponsor row on `kraft` |
| Street A-frame | 24 × 36 in | `deep` ground, lake-wave pattern at 20% · reverse lockup · day · time in League Gothic at ≥ 4 in cap height · a big `heirloom` arrow |
| Info-tent banner | 3 × 8 ft | `heirloom` ground, awning stripe along the bottom edge, emblem at left, "INFO · TOKENS · LOST & FOUND" |
| Stall card | 8.5 × 5.5 in | Category tint ground with white sunburst · vendor name in League Gothic · stall number in a `deep` pill · a "SNAP/EBT" solid badge where it applies |
| Market Match sign | 11 × 17 in | `sage` ground, three numbered steps, card icon, the Bay Badge small at the bottom |
| Sub-brand poster | 11 × 17 in | The ticket mark leads, on its identity colour; the Bay Badge sits at the foot |

## Rules

- Print colours come from the Daylight hex values. Proof `corn` on uncoated stock (it drifts green) and `deep` (it can go muddy, so add 10% black if needed).
- Keep type at 24 pt or larger on anything read at a distance. Never set a sign in `caption` or `ink-muted`.
- Use one pattern per piece (awning *or* waves) and one script line per piece.
- Always include what, when (day, date and time), where, and the URL.
- Sponsor logos go in one row on `kraft`, one-colour where possible, and never larger than the Market mark.
- For outdoor pieces, use matte coroplast or vinyl. Avoid gloss, which glares in morning sun.
