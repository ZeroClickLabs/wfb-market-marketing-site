# Partners, sponsors & vendors

## Sponsor recognition

- Sponsors appear in a "Thanks to our neighbours" band on `kraft`, introduced by a script eyebrow, with logos in equal-height slots (48px on the web, 1 in in print) and `space-6` gaps.
- Show each sponsor's logo exactly as supplied. Never recolour it into Market colours, and never make it larger than the Market mark.
- Name the tiers as text in `eyebrow` style. Don't signal tiers by logo size beyond two steps.

## Co-branding

- The Market leads. The partner appears as "with [Partner]" in `body-s`, or as their logo in the sponsor band.
- On joint pieces, use the lockup, not the badge, beside a partner logo, and separate the two with a 2px `ink` rule.

## Vendors

- Vendors may use the phrase "Find us at the Whitefish Bay Farmers Market" and the icon mark, unaltered, and may not imply the Market endorses a product.
- The Market supplies stall cards (see Print & signage) so the aisles read as one family.
