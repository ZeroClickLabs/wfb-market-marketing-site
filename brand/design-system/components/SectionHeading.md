SectionHeading opens every section: a tilted script eyebrow, a League Gothic title and the sprig ornament, with an optional lead.

- **Provide:** `title`, optional `eyebrow` (≤ 5 words, decorative), `lead`, `align` (`center` default or `left`), `as` (heading level, default `h2`), `ornament={false}`.
- On `deep` sections, wrap the section in `.wfb-on-deep` so the colours switch to `on-deep`, `corn` and `glass`.
