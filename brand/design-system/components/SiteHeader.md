SiteHeader is the top of every page: a `deep` utility row (open status, SNAP/EBT, calendar, social), then the canvas header with the badge breaking below the double rule, up to five nav links and one CTA.

- **Provide:** optional `links` (`{label, href, current}`), `cta` (`{label, href, icon}` or `null`), `status` text, `homeHref`.
- The badge overlaps the next section on purpose, so leave about 60px of calm space at the top of the hero or content.
- Below 900px the links collapse. Pair the header with a menu button in your app shell.
