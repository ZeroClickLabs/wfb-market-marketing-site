SectionEdge is how a page changes ground: the awning scallop under the hero, and the lake wave between bands.

- **Provide:** `kind` (`awning` or `wave`), `tone` (the colour of the edge itself), `alt` (the awning's second stripe), `ground` (the colour behind it, i.e. the next section) and `flip` (wave pointing down).
- Change grounds only through an edge, never with a hard line. Use at most one awning per page, directly under the hero.
