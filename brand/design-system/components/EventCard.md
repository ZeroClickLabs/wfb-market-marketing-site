EventCard is a market ticket: a perforated stub in the market's identity colour with the date, and the title in League Gothic.

- **Provide:** `date` (YYYY-MM-DD), `title`, `market` (`summer` bay, `night` deep, `popup` corn, `winter` beet), `time`, `location`, optional `status` (a Badge), `eyebrow`, `href`.
- The stub always shows the month, day and weekday; the weekday is script, so the full date is also in `dateTime` for screen readers. List tickets in date order.
