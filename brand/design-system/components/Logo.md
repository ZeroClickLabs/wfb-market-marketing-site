Logo renders the Market's marks from the approved artwork: the Bay Badge, the icon, the lockups and the sub-brand tickets.

- **Provide:** `variant` (`emblem` default, `badge` without the ribbon, `icon`, `lockup`, `lockup-reverse`, `night`, `winter`, `popup`), optional `width` (px), `href`, `label`.
- Minimum sizes: emblem 120px, badge 72px, icon 24px, lockup 200px wide. Leave clear space of about 12% of the badge's diameter.
- Use `lockup-reverse` only on `deep` or `bay`. Never recolour, crop, or rebuild a mark; for files outside the UI, use **Assets → Logos**.
