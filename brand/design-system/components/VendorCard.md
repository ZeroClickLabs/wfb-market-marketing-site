VendorCard introduces one vendor in the directory grid: art panel, stamp tags, the name in League Gothic, one line of description, and the town and SNAP badge.

- **Provide:** `name`, `category` or `categories`, `description` (one sentence naming real products), optional `stall`, `from`, `illustration` (a produce name) or `image` + `imageAlt` (a 4:3 photo), `acceptsSnap`, `href`.
- Grid: 1 column on phones, 2 at 640px, 4 at 1200px, with `space-5` gutters.
