Buttons start an action or take the visitor somewhere. They're pills with an ink outline and caps labels, and they lift with a print shadow on hover.

- **Provide:** `children` (a 2–3 word verb phrase in sentence case; the CSS sets the caps), `variant`, optional `size` (`sm`, `md`, `lg`), `icon`/`iconAfter`, `href`.
- `accent` (heirloom) is the one headline action per view. `primary` (bay) is for everyday actions and the header CTA. `outline` is the partner button on light grounds and `inverse` its equivalent on `deep`. `link` is for inline "see all" links.
- Don't put two accent buttons side by side, or use "Click here" or "Submit".
